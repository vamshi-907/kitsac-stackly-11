How our BBQ sauce got its 12-hour smoke

Chef Marco · Jul 12, 2026 · 5 min read

When we started StreetFlame in 2019, our first BBQ sauce was a rushed 30-minute simmer. It was fine. It wasn't great.

Then one weekend, we forgot the pot on low overnight. Twelve hours later, we came back to a sauce that had transformed — deeper, smokier, with a caramelized edge we couldn't replicate any other way.

The Recipe (mostly)

We can't give you everything — but we'll tell you this: it starts with charred tomatoes, molasses, apple cider vinegar, a whisper of espresso, and a hit of chipotle. The magic is the time.

"The best flavor is the flavor you didn't rush." — Chef Marco

Every batch we make now goes on the smoker at 6pm and comes off at 6am the next morning. It's the only way.
