Why we started

From a $2K loan to feeding festivals

StreetFlame started in 2019 with a beat-up van, a portable griddle, and a stubborn belief that street food deserves to be as good as any restaurant. Six years later, we've served over 84,000 meals and catered 400+ events — from backyard birthdays to 5,000-guest music festivals.

Our mission is simple: source the freshest local ingredients, cook everything to order, and never lose the energy of the street.
